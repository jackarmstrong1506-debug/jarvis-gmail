Privacy policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Jarvis is a private tool with a single user, who is also the person who runs it. It has no other users, no customers and no operator other than its owner. This policy describes what it does with data from a Google Account when that account's owner connects it.

What is accessed

With permission, Jarvis reads email from mailboxes the operator explicitly connects. It requests one Google permission, gmail.readonly, which allows it to view messages and settings and nothing else. It cannot send, reply to, forward, modify or delete mail, and it cannot change any account setting. No other Google service or data is requested.

What it is used for

Unread messages are summarised into a short briefing shown only to the operator. That is the only purpose.

Where data is kept

Message content is read when a briefing is produced and is not retained afterwards. The access tokens that permit reading are stored on the operator's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server belonging to this project, because there is no such server.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Email content is not sold, published, transferred or shared with any third party, and is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models. It is not read by any person other than the mailbox's own owner.

Limited use

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing access

Access can be revoked at any time, without reference to this project, from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking it stops all reading immediately and permanently.
