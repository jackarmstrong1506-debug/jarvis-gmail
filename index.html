Jarvis-Gmail

A private personal assistant, used by one person.

Jarvis-Gmail is a personal automation tool. It runs privately on its operator's own computer and helps with day-to-day admin: keeping notes, tracking a small reselling business, and producing a short summary twice a day.

Why it asks for access to Gmail

One of those summaries covers unread email. To write it, Jarvis-Gmail needs to read its operator's own mailboxes. It asks Google for read-only access, which means it can look at messages but cannot send, reply to, alter or delete anything. That limit is enforced by Google, not merely promised here.

Who can use it

Jarvis-Gmail is not a product and is not offered to the public. It connects only to mailboxes belonging to its own operator, who is also the only person who ever sees anything it produces.
